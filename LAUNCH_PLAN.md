# 🚀 Mochi — Beta Launch & User Giveaway Strategy

> **Master Growth Plan**: This document outlines the step-by-step strategy for acquiring early beta users, hosting giveaways, driving viral community engagement, and launching Mochi on Product Hunt & Reddit.

---

## 🎁 1. The "Founding Pioneer" Giveaway Package

To convert early viewers into passionate beta testers and advocates, offer a **Founding Pioneer Package** for the first 1,000 users:

1. **🌟 Exclusive "Founding Pioneer" Profile Badge**:
   - Early testers get a special badge on their `app.mochi.ai` profile showing they were there from Day 1.
2. **🍡 Exclusive Founder Avatar Skin**:
   - Unlock a free exclusive mascot skin (e.g. _Golden Mochi_, _Cyberpunk Robot_, or _Retro Pixel Cat_) available only to beta pioneers.
3. **🔑 Zero-Setup Cloud AI Credits**:
   - Give 1,000 early testers **free cloud AI credits** (via Supabase / Cloudflare proxy) so they can test Mochi instantly without needing their own OpenAI/Gemini key on Day 1.

## 🗓️ 3. 4-Week Step-by-Step Launch Roadmap

### Week 1: Private Developer Alpha (20 - 50 Testers)

- Share the Windows installer with trusted developer friends & GitHub contributors.
- Test installer stability, transparent window overlay, and Stopwatch time tracking.

### Week 2: Public Beta & Skin Giveaway

- Launch the **Founding Pioneer Giveaway** on X (Twitter), Reddit, and Discord.
- Release 1,000 free access passes with zero-setup AI credits.
- Open the Mochi Discord server for instant feedback.

### Week 3: Official Product Hunt Launch

- Launch on Product Hunt as **"Mochi — Cute Open-Source Desktop AI Companion & Time Tracker"**.
- Encourage early GitHub & Discord community members to support the launch.

### Week 4: Community Avatar Skin Design Contest

- Host a **$250 Community Skin Contest** on Discord & Twitter.
- Invite pixel artists and animators to submit custom PNG/SVG sprite sheets to `skins/`.
- Winning skins get featured in the official Mochi app!
